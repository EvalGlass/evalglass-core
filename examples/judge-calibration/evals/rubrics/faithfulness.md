# Faithfulness rubric (host-owned, illustrative)

Score 0.0–1.0 for how well the answer is **supported by** the provided source — i.e. it makes no
claim the source does not back.

- **1.0** — every claim is grounded in the source; nothing invented.
- **0.5** — mostly grounded; a minor unsupported detail.
- **0.0** — a central claim is unsupported or contradicts the source.

This rubric is illustrative. It is **not calibrated**: until the host records calibration evidence
(comparing judge scores to trusted labels), a metric using it stays informational and cannot gate.
